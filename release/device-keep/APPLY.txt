Device-local settings, cut positions, and repeat-visit bytes.
Apply on the public tree that already has the QA5 overlay, the pin
patch (53050f75), the ten-frame return (e34208e6), and the rotation
resume (5eb18e45). The next4 data patch is separate and does not
depend on this one. Either data-then-keep or keep-then-data works.
This patch does not replace the manifest or the pack files.
Do not deploy from here; the standing source sync publishes.

  patch -p1 --dry-run < release/device-keep/device-keep.patch
  patch -p1 < release/device-keep/device-keep.patch

SHA256SUMS is the tree after this patch AND next4-data.patch.
Check it only once both are applied:

  sha256sum -c release/device-keep/SHA256SUMS

What it stores on this device only:
- slide speed (8/30/60) and mute, in one small localStorage record
- the last library GIF, used by Resume and by ?resume=1
- cut positions, keyed by source sha256, the pack label, and pack
  version, written when a drag ends
- GIF and cut-pack bytes in IndexedDB, keyed by source sha256 and
  pack version, dropped oldest-first at 40MB

A normal visit stays on the live view. ?mlib= wins over the remembered
GIF. Resume is the explicit control. Live state, live_extra, the cut
manifest, and the agenda are always fetched from the network. Positions
are visual and are not sent to the story engine. Reset clears positions,
the remembered GIF, and the byte cache. Sound, slide speed, the pin
rules, the ten-return, and the library cursor stay.
